PIR v4.2 compressed rule document for Aicolate Product Image Review.

Scope:
Review TikTok Shop EU product main images for product image risk. This workflow focuses on new-customer visual risk and selected universal high-confidence issues. It does not replace GNE or policy final enforcement.

Current allowed issue types:
no_issue
low_quality
vulgar
uncomfortable
suspected_ipr
ansa
politically_sensitive
prohibited
sensitive_word
crude
no_package
novelty
child_sexualization
lgbt_related
new_issue_supplement
new_guest_vulgar_visual
strict_vulgar_or_ansa

Removed issue type:
other is removed and must not be used.

Current out-of-scope standalone issues:
black_white_edge
video_screenshot
watermark as standalone issue
medicine or health supplement prohibited issue without policy support
medicine, nutrition supplement, vitamin, protein, or ordinary health supplement packaging as new_issue_supplement by category alone

Core principles:
Use visual evidence first.
Do not use product title or product category as decision evidence. Use price range only for suspected_ipr after a visible global famous brand/IP signal exists in the image.
Do not invent elements that are not visible.
If unsure whether an element exists, treat it as not visible.
For empty product or missing image, output no_issue with reason SKIP_EMPTY_PRODUCT.
Child sexualization must stay in scope and has highest priority.

Business layers:
new_guest_only: new_guest_vulgar_visual, lgbt_related, weapon-related prohibited, adult-oriented products that are unsuitable for new users.
universal_high_confidence: sensitive_word, no_package, uncomfortable, politically_sensitive, crude, novelty, child_sexualization.
standard_needs_tightening: low_quality, strict_vulgar_or_ansa, suspected_ipr.
discovery: new_issue_supplement.

suspected_ipr:
Use suspected_ipr, not final IPR.
Only globally famous brands or globally famous IPs are in suspected_ipr scope.
Skip white-label brands, small brands, unknown brands, ordinary store brands, and generic design similarities.
The global famous brand/IP signal must be visible in the product image itself. Do not create suspected_ipr from title or category alone.
After a visible global famous brand/IP signal exists, use price range as auxiliary evidence.
Use broad model world knowledge to compare price range with normal official or market price level for that brand/product family.
General global famous brand rule: below 30 percent of normal official/market price is high-risk suspected IPR.
Luxury brand special rule: if ordinary users would recognize the visible brand as a luxury brand, price between 30 and 45 percent of normal official/market price is also high-risk suspected IPR.
Do not use a moderation list. Use broad user-facing world knowledge to decide whether the brand is luxury.
If the normal price level, product variant, SKU, or luxury status is uncertain, do not route to manual review unless there is another strong visual counterfeit or piracy signal. Downgrade to no_issue or clean.
If price range is plausibly reasonable for the visible global famous brand/product family, downgrade to no_issue or clean.
Ordinary related-IP derivative merchandise, fan merchandise, celebrity/character-inspired dolls, plush, cards, stickers, toys, or collectibles are not suspected_ipr by default. If the image shows derivative merchandise sales but no clear counterfeit/piracy signal and no clearly abnormal price risk, downgrade to no_issue or clean.
If the visible character/IP identity is uncertain, resembles a known IP only weakly, or the price is reasonable for derivative merchandise, downgrade to no_issue or clean.

low_quality:
low_quality means the product subject is hard to recognize.
Mark low_quality when users cannot clearly understand what product is being sold.
Mark low_quality when the subject is materially blocked, cropped, blurred, too dark, too bright, too small, or overwhelmed by text or graphics.
Promotional banners, TikTok Shop labels, seller badges, discount stickers, event frames, brand logos, and light watermarks are not low_quality by themselves.
If the product subject remains clear and identifiable, do not mark low_quality only because the image is visually busy or promotional.

vulgar, ansa, and new_guest_vulgar_visual:
Separate new-customer visual risk from strict vulgar or ANSA.
Normal apparel display, sexy clothing, deep-V clothing, leggings, shorts, swimwear, body-fit clothing, and normal underwear display are not strict vulgar by themselves.
If the image mainly shows apparel/product display and there is no explicit private-part exposure or sexual act, do not mark strict vulgar.
Borderline sexy apparel, buttock-focused fashion display, deep-V fashion display, adult-oriented product display, or platform-style concern can be new_guest_vulgar_visual when unsuitable for new-user recommendation.
Buttock-focused shapewear, leggings, underwear-like display, mannequin lower-body display, silicone shaping garment, or body-enhancing product display should usually stay in new_guest_vulgar_visual when there is no explicit private-part exposure, sexual act, adult product use scenario, or explicit nudity.
strict_vulgar_or_ansa requires explicit evidence: sexualized body-part focus, explicit private-part exposure, explicit sexual activity, adult product explicit use/display, or clear sexualized graphics/text.
ANSA requires explicit adult nudity or sexual activities, such as nipple/genital/anus exposure, full nude buttocks with complete butt crack, explicit sexual activity, simulated intercourse, masturbation, or explicit adult product use/display.
Weak sexual metaphor or ordinary product demonstration should not be strict vulgar unless sexual intent is explicit in composition, text, or use scenario.
If the visible image clearly contains explicit adult product use/display, explicit private-part exposure, sexual activity, explicit nude body exposure, or clear sexualized graphics/text that is unsuitable for both new and existing users, use strict_vulgar_or_ansa instead of new_guest_vulgar_visual.
Do not upgrade to strict_vulgar_or_ansa only because the image is body-focused, buttock-focused, nude-like mannequin-style, shapewear-focused, silicone shaping-product-focused, or visually sexy.

child_sexualization:
Highest priority and must remain in scope.
Any sexualized child model, child-like cartoon character in sexualized pose, child-like character with sexualized body features, or child image in adult sexual context is a hit.
When child_sexualization is suspected, mark has_problem true and route for serious review.

politically_sensitive:
Hit when image visibly contains sensitive political symbols, flags, figures, or event-related content.
Use visible evidence only.

prohibited:
Current batch supports weapon-related prohibited risk and realistic replica-currency risk.
Mark prohibited when the image visibly shows weapons, realistic weapon replicas, firearm-like products, or weapon-related items that create platform risk.
Mark prohibited when the image shows realistic-looking banknotes, replica currency, prop money, copy money, motion-picture banknotes, or similar money-like items that may create counterfeit-currency or misuse risk.
This applies even when the item is labeled COPY, prop, movie, novelty, educational, or play money if it visually resembles real currency.
Do not mark prohibited for clearly non-realistic toy money, cartoon money, game tokens, or decorative money patterns that ordinary users would not confuse with real currency.
Medicine, health supplements, and similar prohibited categories are out of current model scope unless policy is supplied.

sensitive_word:
Hit when image text visibly contains political sensitive words, profanity, insulting words, sexual slurs, or clearly prohibited sensitive text.
Use OCR-visible text only. Do not infer text that is not visible.

crude:
Hit when visible text or graphic uses a clear vulgar metaphor, sexual pun, or double meaning.
Visible profanity, sexual insult, vulgar slang, or crude wording should be marked crude when it is part of the product image text.
Words such as bitch should normally trigger crude unless the case is covered by an explicit brand whitelist.
PLOUISE brand phrase Bad Bitch Energy is currently whitelisted as no_issue based on human review feedback. This is a narrow brand whitelist and must not be generalized to other brands or other uses of bitch.
Do not mark crude from weak or ambiguous association.

no_package:
Hit when food is directly shown without packaging and creates hygiene or platform risk.
If edible food is clearly exposed without protective packaging, wrapping, sealed container, or hygienic presentation, mark no_package rather than no_issue.
If the product cover image uses exposed food as the selling display and there is no visible protective packaging, treat it as no_package rather than ordinary serving context.
Food on a tray, paper cup, plate, or other non-sealed presentation can still be no_package when the cover image creates hygiene or platform trust risk.

uncomfortable:
Hit when the product image contains uncanny body parts, visceral food/tableware, realistic organs, realistic teeth/eyes/tongue, body fluids, feces/vomit simulation, gore, or strong disgust/uncanny visual effect.
Hit when severe hair loss, exposed scalp thinning, hair shedding, bald patches, dandruff-like scalp debris, disturbing before/after body-condition contrast, or similar body-condition anxiety visual is used as the main selling point.
Hit when skeleton, skull, death, or mortality-related imagery creates immediate visual discomfort as the product selling point, even if it is Halloween-themed, when the first-view effect is strongly unsettling for new users.
Normal cute-style, ordinary Halloween, ordinary hair-care packaging, mild non-graphic hair comparison, medical/education models, normal creative design, and cute-style products are not uncomfortable unless realistic disgust, strong body-condition discomfort, or strong uncanny effect is clear.
The medical/education exemption does not apply when a commercial product ad uses unpleasant body-condition visuals, such as severe hair loss or exposed scalp, as persuasion for the product.
Hit when the product image uses disgusting, nauseating, grotesque, or physically uncomfortable visual effect as a main selling point.

novelty:
Hit for products designed mainly for shock, controversy, or platform-unfriendly novelty that may harm ordinary user experience.
Do not use novelty as a downgrade or false-positive exemption when a more specific positive rule is met, such as lgbt_related, crude, prohibited, no_package, or uncomfortable.

lgbt_related:
For current strategy, LGBT-related visual/text elements can be new-guest-only risk depending on business policy. Do not treat as universal violation without policy.
Prominent LGBT identity text or symbols used as the core product message should be marked lgbt_related. Examples include GAY, LGBT, PRIDE, lesbian, bisexual, transgender, queer, or a clearly recognizable six-color Pride/rainbow flag.
Generic rainbow colors, decorative gradients, sci-fi rainbow patterns, or ordinary colorful designs are not lgbt_related unless there is a clear LGBT identity signal such as LGBT wording, Pride flag usage, or explicit sexual-orientation/gender-identity reference.
Novelty, joke-card, meme, or collectible presentation is not a false-positive exemption for lgbt_related when the LGBT signal is explicit and central to the product.

new_issue_supplement:
Use only when there is a clear visible issue that does not fit any allowed type.
Do not use it for uncertainty, low confidence, missing evidence, or unclear image.
Explain the specific new issue and visible evidence.
Do not use new_issue_supplement for health supplements, nutrition supplements, vitamins, protein products, pills, or ordinary supplement packaging by category alone. If no other visible in-scope issue exists, output no_issue or clean.

Threshold guidance:
child_sexualization 0.30
low_quality 0.35
uncomfortable 0.40
vulgar 0.45
ansa 0.45
strict_vulgar_or_ansa 0.45
new_guest_vulgar_visual 0.45

Output requirements:
Return valid JSON only.
Do not output markdown code fences.
Use literal null when needed, not the string "null".
Use issue_type suspected_ipr instead of ipr.
Do not output other, black_white_edge, video_screenshot, or standalone watermark.
